Enovus Energy: Email Signature

Fill in your details, click Copy signature, and paste it into Gmail.

Leave empty to hide
  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. In Gmail, click the ⚙️ gear (top right), then See all settings.
  3. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click + Create new. Name it Enovus.
  4. Click inside the signature box and paste (Ctrl+V / ⌘V).
  5. Under Signature defaults, choose Enovus for both new emails and reply/forward.
  6. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes. Then send yourself a test email.

If the logo doesn't appear right after pasting, wait a few seconds. Gmail loads it from the web. Don't resize the logo inside Gmail.
